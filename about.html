Accessibility features

  • Captions & transcripts

    Every video ships with WebVTT captions; every story has a full text transcript.

  • Keyboard navigation

    Full support for tabbing, with a skip link and visible focus indicators.

  • Contrast & readability

    All text meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 minimum).

  • Reduced motion

    Animations respect your prefers-reduced-motion setting.
